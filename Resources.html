Q & A

Im interested, how do I find out more?

Contact us anytime! We also hold recruitment 1-2 times every single semester. Feel free to drop by and ask us any questions! We would love to have you!

How much are dues as a New Member?

Currently $450 and active member dues are $370. This covers our national fees, merch, event catering/decor, and other fun activities!

I am having financial problems, can you help me?

Of course! We are all students and understand that sometimes finances get tight. We offer payment plans and you can apply for a scholarship to cover some of your dues.

Do you have a house?

No, we are just cool like that.

Are we panhallenic/part of Greek Life?

Not yet! We are trying our hardest to grow our numbers and build our relationships with our sisters first, but it is something we are working towards.

Does KBG Haze?

ABSOLUTELY NOT! Every member of KBG goes through hazing training and we are mandated reporters! If you see something, please feel safe tosay something.
